01 / THE PROBLEM

Security has a visibility problem.

Attack attempts, defensive decisions, and final outcomes are often scattered across tools. Teams can disagree about what happened—and the evidence may not be independently verifiable.

ISOLATEDEVAL turns that fragmented sequence into one observable, auditable run.

02 / COMMIT–REVEAL

Keep secrets secret. Until it's time.

The Red Team commits an attack payload first. The Blue Team sees counters and defence configuration—not the payload text. When the run concludes, results can be released for review.

The interface makes isolation visible through locked panels, separate counters, and a reveal animation.

04 / ONE OPERATING SURFACE

Five roles. One shared truth.

Clients upload assets to inspect. Red Team commits and reveals. Blue Team tunes defensive rules without seeing attack content. Admins conclude runs and verify evidence. Auditors review the trail.

Role-aware UI improves clarity. The backend must still enforce every permission on every request.
